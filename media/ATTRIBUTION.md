# Sample video

`sample-crosswalk.mp4` / `.webm` are edited from **person-bicycle-car-detection.mp4** in the
[Intel IoT DevKit sample-videos](https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos) collection,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: clips were trimmed and reordered, the walker was composited into the car scenes
(background subtraction + alignment) so pedestrians and cars share the same moments, and
one frame was held so the second car appears to wait. It is a demonstration clip, not a
record of a real incident.
